# The job is exam prep, not a general second brain

The Brain exists so one Owner can pass an Exam: remember Points, drill questions, and get through that Exam. We rejected a general “store anything and retrieve it later” second-brain as the job. The service can still be named Brain; the work is exam prep. Skills should expose exam work, not a generic notes API.

Superseded: the Owner revised the job to assisting learning under a Direction, not binding to an Exam.
